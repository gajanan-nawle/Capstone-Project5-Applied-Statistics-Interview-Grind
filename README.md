# Applied Business Analytics – End Course Summative Assessment

## 📄 Project Overview
**Phase 1: Technical Interview Repository**
This project serves as a comprehensive summative assessment designed to simulate the rigors of a technical Data Analyst interview. The goal was to demonstrate readiness for the competitive analytics market by solving high-impact questions and communicating complex statistical concepts to stakeholders.

### 🎯 Problem Statement
**Problem:** In the field of Data Analytics, technical skills alone are insufficient. Analysts often struggle to translate complex statistical theory into actionable business strategy for non-technical stakeholders.

**Goal:** To bridge this gap by solving **50 critical interview questions** selected from a pool of 80. The objective is to demonstrate not just mathematical proficiency, but the ability to apply these concepts to real-world business challenges.

**Requirement:** The project requires a **15-minute video presentation** to verbally articulate five complex topics from **Error Analysis** to **A/B Testing** validating the ability to communicate technical insights effectively.

## 📚 Technical Assessment (50 Questions)
I have curated and solved 50 interview questions covering the following domains:

* **Linear Algebra:** Vectors, Scalars, and Matrix operations.
* **Probability Theory:** Conditional Probability, Bayes Theorem, and Random Variables.
* **Descriptive Statistics:** Measures of Central Tendency, Skewness, Kurtosis, and Outlier detection.
* **Inferential Statistics:** Sampling distributions, Central Limit Theorem, and Confidence Intervals.
* **Hypothesis Testing:** P-values, Significance levels, and Experiment Design.

* **Statistical Analysis:** Proficiency in interpreting data distributions and error metrics.
* **Strategic Communication:** Ability to translate math into business strategy.
